How PARSE works

Each stage has one job, a defined input, and a validated output — so the AI reasons inside a structure instead of free-associating at you.

  1. 1

    Ingest

    INPUT

    Your description comes in as plain text and gets checked for enough substance to work with. No AI call.

  2. 2

    Structure

    UNDERSTAND

    Industry, pain points, goals, and constraints are pulled out into a consistent schema.

  3. 3

    Recommend

    RECOMMEND

    Ranked suggestions, each required to cite the specific pain point or goal it addresses.

  4. 4

    Blueprint

    DESIGN

    The one you pick becomes components, a workflow, and an architecture diagram.

  5. 5

    Export

    EXPORT

    Everything assembled into a Markdown document you can download and hand off.
